About Us

Who are we?

Evergreen Energy is a sustainable energy solutions company. We believe clean energy should be simple, affordable, and available to everyone, and we are dedicated to helping homes, businesses, and communities move away from fossil fuels and toward renewable power.

Our Mission & Vision

At Evergreen Energy, our mission is to make renewable power the foundation of everyday life. We are driven by the belief that clean energy should not be a luxury, but a right accessible to all households, businesses, and communities. Our vision is a future where fossil fuels are replaced by sustainable solutions that empower people to live affordably, responsibly, and in harmony with the environment. By combining innovation with integrity, we aim to lead the transition toward a greener world, ensuring that every project we deliver contributes to reducing carbon emissions and building a resilient energy future for generations to come.

What Do We Do?

We provide practical, cost‑effective solutions that help Australians embrace renewable energy and reduce their dependence on fossil fuels. Since 2020, our team has worked alongside homeowners, businesses, and community groups to design and implement projects that deliver real impact. From solar installations and wind power systems to energy‑efficient upgrades and comprehensive energy audits, we tailor our services to meet diverse needs while lowering costs and improving sustainability. By combining technical expertise with a commitment to transparency, we make the shift to clean energy simple, affordable, and rewarding for everyone we serve.

Commitment to Sustainability

At Evergreen Energy, sustainability is at the heart of everything we do. We integrate eco‑friendly practices into every project, ensuring that our solutions not only deliver reliable energy but also protect the planet for future generations. This commitment reinforces our brand identity and drives our mission to build a greener tomorrow.

Acknowledgement of Country

We acknowledge the Traditional Custodians of the lands on which we work and live, and pay our respects to Elders past and present. Sovereignty was never ceded.

Every solar farm, wind farm and battery we build sits on Country that Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples have cared for over tens of thousands of years. Before a project begins, we seek the free, prior and informed consent of Traditional Owners, and we work with them on land use, cultural heritage protection, local jobs and shared benefits, because a clean energy future is only just if it respects the people and places it depends on.
