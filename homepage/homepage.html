About Us

Who are we?

Evergreen Energy At Sustainable Energy Solutions Company, we believe clean energy should be simple, affordable, and available to everyone. We are dedicated to helping homes, businesses, and communities move away from fossil fuels and toward renewable power. Since 2020, we have helped Australians cut their energy costs and reduce their carbon footprint with solutions like solar installation, wind power, energy-efficient upgrades, energy audits.

What do we do?

We plan and deliver wind, solar and battery storage projects. Our technology team turns that work into clear, accessible websites, so communities can see what is being built, how it performs and how to have their say.

Our Mission & Vision

Evergreen Energy is committed to delivering clean, affordable power solutions while shaping a sustainable future for communities worldwide. Our vision is a future where renewable energy empowers every home, business, and community to thrive responsibly.

Commitment to Sustainability

At Evergreen Energy, sustainability is at the heart of everything we do. We integrate eco‑friendly practices into every project, ensuring that our solutions not only deliver reliable energy but also protect the planet for future generations. This commitment reinforces our brand identity and drives our mission to build a greener tomorrow.
