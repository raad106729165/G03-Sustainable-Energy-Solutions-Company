Our Group

We are Group G03, five students who came together to build the careers website for Evergreen Energy, a clean energy company growing its technology team. Each of us owned one part of the site, and we shared one brand, one stylesheet and one goal: a site that makes applying for a clean energy career simple.

How We Worked Together

  1. Plan: we agreed on the company, brand colours, logo and page list, and gave each member one page to own.
  2. Build: each member worked on their own branch in our shared GitHub repository, so nobody overwrote anyone else's work.
  3. Share: Marwa's header and footer and one shared stylesheet keep every page looking like one website.
  4. Merge: finished pages were merged into the main branch, then checked together for consistency and validation.
