Build Your Career With Us

At Evergreen Energy Sustainable Energy Solutions, we believe technology and innovation can help create a cleaner future. We support sustainable energy solutions and use digital technology to share information about renewable energy projects.

We are looking for talented people who want to help us build accessible websites and inclusive digital experiences that inform the public about clean energy and community programs.

Why Join Us?

  • Flexible hybrid work arrangements.
  • Five paid volunteering days each year for environmental projects.
  • An annual budget for learning, training and certifications.

Inclusive Employment

We encourage applications from Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples. We aim to work respectfully with First Nations communities on renewable energy projects on Country and value their knowledge and perspectives.

Front-End Web Developer

Position Details

Reference Number:
WD101
Salary:
AUD $85,000–$100,000 per year, plus superannuation
Reporting Line:
Head of Digital Platforms

About the Role

Build and maintain our public websites so people can learn about our clean energy solutions, follow our solar and wind projects, and join community programs. You will turn designs into fast, accessible and responsive web pages.

Key Responsibilities

  1. Build responsive web pages from design mock-ups using semantic HTML5 and CSS3.
  2. Keep project information pages up to date, including project locations, capacity and progress.
  3. Make sure pages follow WCAG 2.2 AA guidelines so people with disabilities can use them.
  4. Improve page speed and reduce unnecessary resource use through lightweight code and optimised images.
  5. Use Git and GitHub to manage code and review teammates' work.
  6. Work with designers and the communications team on public campaigns.

Essential Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in IT, Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent experience.
  • At least two years of front-end development experience.
  • Strong skills in HTML5, CSS3 and responsive design.
  • Working knowledge of web accessibility standards.
  • Experience with Git version control.

Preferable Requirements

  • Experience with JavaScript frameworks such as React or Vue.
  • Knowledge of sustainable web design practices.
  • Experience with a content management system such as WordPress.
  • Interest in renewable energy or climate action.

UX/UI Designer

Position Details

Reference Number:
UX202
Salary:
AUD $90,000–$110,000 per year, plus superannuation
Reporting Line:
Head of Digital Platforms

About the Role

Design clear, inclusive digital experiences that help the public understand clean energy and take part in community programs. You will research user needs and design layouts for our websites and engagement tools.

Key Responsibilities

  1. Run user research and usability testing with community members and customers.
  2. Create wireframes, prototypes and final designs for web pages and online forms.
  3. Design clear visual displays of energy data, including savings calculators and project dashboards.
  4. Maintain the company design system so pages look consistent and on-brand.
  5. Design for accessibility, including colour contrast, readable text and keyboard use.
  6. Hand finished designs over to developers and support them during implementation.

Essential Requirements

  • Degree in Design, Human-Computer Interaction or a related field.
  • At least three years of UX/UI design experience.
  • Skills in Figma or a similar design tool.
  • A portfolio demonstrating user-centred design work.
  • Understanding of inclusive and accessible design.

Preferable Requirements

  • Experience designing data visualisations.
  • Basic knowledge of HTML and CSS.
  • Experience with community or public-sector engagement projects.
  • Knowledge of the renewable energy industry.
