Renewable Energy Project Engineer

Position Details

Reference Number:
RE201
Salary:
AUD $95,000 to $115,000 per year, plus superannuation
Reporting Line:
Director of Operations

About the Role

Lead the design and implementation of solar and wind energy projects across Australia. You will work closely with contractors, local councils, and community groups to deliver sustainable energy solutions that reduce carbon emissions and improve energy efficiency.

Key Responsibilities

  1. Design and oversee renewable energy installations including solar farms and wind turbines.
  2. Conduct feasibility studies and prepare technical reports for stakeholders.
  3. Ensure compliance with Australian energy regulations and safety standards.
  4. Collaborate with project managers and engineers to meet deadlines and budgets.
  5. Monitor system performance and recommend improvements.

Essential Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in Electrical, Mechanical, or Renewable Energy Engineering.
  • Minimum three years of project engineering experience.
  • Strong knowledge of solar PV and wind energy systems.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork skills.

Preferable Requirements

  • Experience with energy storage solutions.
  • Knowledge of sustainable construction practices.
  • Interest in community-based renewable projects.

Data Analyst – Energy Systems

Position Details

Reference Number:
DA301
Salary:
AUD $75,000 to $90,000 per year, plus superannuation
Reporting Line:
Head of Analytics

About the Role

Analyse energy production and consumption data to help optimise renewable energy systems. You will provide insights that guide decision-making, improve efficiency, and support the company’s mission of delivering affordable clean energy solutions.

Key Responsibilities

  1. Collect and analyse data from solar, wind, and storage systems.
  2. Develop dashboards and reports for management and stakeholders.
  3. Identify trends and recommend strategies to improve efficiency.
  4. Work with engineers to validate system performance data.
  5. Ensure data accuracy and maintain secure databases.

Essential Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in Data Science, Statistics, or a related field.
  • Proficiency in SQL and data visualisation tools.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to communicate insights clearly to non-technical audiences.

Preferable Requirements

  • Experience with Python or R for data analysis.
  • Knowledge of renewable energy systems.
  • Interest in sustainability and climate action.
